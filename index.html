Dil Se Maafi (Bestfriend)

Pichle 3 mahino se roz subah uth kar sabse pehla khyal yahi aata hai ki 'kash us din maine woh baat apne tak hi rakhi hoti.' Sach kahun toh, yeh 3 mahine kisi saza se kam nahi kate hain. Tumne jab se baat band ki hai, lagta hai jaise mere din ka sabse zaroori hissa kahin gayab ho gaya ho.

Main roz sochta hu ki tumhe message karu, kuch bolu, par fir samajh nahi aata ki kya kahun kyunki galti itni saaf thi ki safayi dene ka bhi haq nahi bachta tha. Par yaar, ab is khamoshi ke saath aur nahi jiya jaa raha.

Mujhe achhi tarah yaad hai tumne kitne bharose ke saath kaha tha ki kisiko matt batana ki hum best friends hain. Aur maine? Maine us bharose ki dhajjiyan uda di. Chahe anjaane me hua ho ya bevkufi me, galti toh meri hi thi na. Mujhe aaj tak samajh nahi aata ki main us din ilta be-akal kaise ho gaya tha.

Tumhe pata hai, sabse zyada takleef kis baat ki hoti hai? Yeh soch kar ki jis insaan ke liye main sabse safe jagah hona chahiye tha, wahi insaan mere hi kisi harkat ki wajah se hurt ho gaya. Tumne mujhse baat karna band kar diya, woh tumhara gussa tha, aur woh bilkul theek tha.

Par is 3 mahine ki doori ne mujhe ek baat toh sikha di hai—ki meri life me tumhari dosti ki jagah koi aur kabhi nahi le sakta. Maaf kar do na yaar. Ek aakhri baar pooch raha hu... kya ek mauka nahi mil sakta sab kuch theek karne ka? Please.
